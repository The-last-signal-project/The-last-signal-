### 1. Objective

The objective of **The Last Signal Online** is to create an immersive post-apocalyptic survival MMORPG in which players explore a persistent world shaped by the consequences of a scientific catastrophe.

The project aims to provide a long-lasting, evolving, and cooperative adventure where every player can create their own story.

---

### 2. Global Vision

**The Last Signal Online** is designed to be a living game.

The world continues to evolve even when players are offline.

Player choices, global events, and game updates will gradually shape and transform the universe.

The project is designed to be developed over several years, with new content regularly added and expanded.

---

### 3. Game Pillars

Development is built around six fundamental pillars.

#### 🌍 Exploration

Players will discover a vast open world consisting of diverse regions, abandoned cities, secret laboratories, military bases, and contaminated areas.

Each area has its own history, dangers, and resources.

---

#### 🤝 Cooperation

Cooperation is at the heart of the experience.

Players can:

* form groups;
* join guilds;
* complete dungeons;
* trade resources;
* participate in global events.

---

#### ⚔️ Combat

Combat is designed to be strategic.

Players will need to prepare their equipment, manage their resources, and adapt their strategies according to the enemies they encounter.

---

#### 📈 Progression

Progression is based on several systems:

* experience;
* skills;
* equipment;
* professions;
* reputation.

Each player will be able to develop their character according to their own playstyle.

---

#### 🌍 Persistent World

The world is shared by all players.

Players' actions can permanently influence certain areas or events, allowing the world to evolve over time.

---

#### 📖 Immersion

The story is revealed gradually.

Players discover the narrative by exploring the world, completing quests, and uncovering documents left behind before the catastrophe.

---

### 4. Player Experience

We want players to experience:

* the tension of survival;
* the satisfaction of making progress;
* the enjoyment of cooperating with others;
* the desire to explore;
* the curiosity to uncover the world's secrets.

---

### 5. Project Values

The development of the game is based on several core principles:

* Accessibility
* Cooperation
* Immersion
* Freedom of exploration
* Rewarding progression
* Constant evolution
* Respect for the community

---

### 6. Long-Term Goals

In the long term, **The Last Signal Online** aims to provide:

* a vast persistent world;
* several hundred concurrent players;
* regular content updates;
* a player-driven economy;
* global world events;
* story-driven expansions.

---

### 7. Target Audience

The game is intended for players who enjoy:

* MMORPGs;
* survival games;
* open-world games;
* exploration;
* cooperative gameplay;
* long-term progression.

**Recommended age:** **12+**
