# 04 — Chronology

## 1. Introduction

The chronology of **The Last Signal** establishes the major events of the game universe in strict temporal order.

It serves as the common design and narrative reference for story development, worldbuilding, character arcs, factions, and dynamic world events encountered by players.

> **Design Principle:** The timeline must distinguish between the objective historical events of the universe, the knowledge available to in-world characters, and the narrative discoveries revealed progressively to the player.

---

## 2. Pre-Catastrophe Era

Prior to the start of the game's timeline, a series of scientific and geopolitical events gradually led civilization toward the collapse:

This period contextualizes:

* the current fractured state of the world;
* the emergence of the major survival factions;
* the root causes of factional warfare;
* rapid scientific breakthroughs in genetics and military artificial intelligence;
* the specific events leading to the player's initial awakening.

Detailed canon entries for this era are added iteratively as lore development progresses.

---

## 3. The Trigger Event

The main campaign begins when a catastrophic event destabilizes the post-war world order.

This event is the starting catalyst of the main plotline and allows the player to progressively understand:

1. what transpired in the final days of the pre-EMP era;
2. why the daily radio broadcast commenced;
3. the environmental and social consequences unfolding across the continent;
4. what decisive role the player can play in shaping the future.

Definitive scenario specifics are established in the core campaign documentation.

---

## 4. The Awakening & Narrative Discovery

The player is introduced to the world in its current devastated state.

At this initial stage, the player possesses only limited fragmented information about the broader crisis.

The narrative architecture is structured across six progressive stages:

$$\textbf{Discovery} \longrightarrow \textbf{Questioning} \longrightarrow \textbf{Investigation} \longrightarrow \textbf{Revelation} \longrightarrow \textbf{Comprehension} \longrightarrow \textbf{Consequences}$$

Certain sensitive truths are intentionally withheld early on to preserve the world's central mysteries.

---

## 5. Chronological Narrative Phases

### Phase I — Discovery

The player encounters:

* the immediate starting environment and survival basics;
* baseline lore regarding local settlements;
* key initial NPCs and survival mentors;
* initial low-level mutant threats;
* the first decrypted fragments regarding the Lighthouse signal.

---

### Phase II — Investigation

The player begins investigating the source of the radio transmissions:

* progressively uncovering that introductory rumors only represent a fraction of the truth;
* discovering contradictory accounts between rival survivor and bandit factions;
* discovering hidden pre-war bunkers and scientific archives.

---

### Phase III — Revelations

The player connects previously isolated plot points:

* discovering the true origin and purpose of the Prometheus AI;
* re-evaluating the true motivations of major faction leaders;
* uncovering Dr. Voss's role in the genetic contamination;
* understanding their own unique physiological immunity.

---

### Phase IV — Escalation & Confrontation

The ramifications of the player's discoveries can no longer be contained:

* factions mobilize for control over the Lighthouse;
* open warfare and territorial skirmishes erupt across regional biomes;
* player choices directly influence faction strength and balance of power.

---

### Phase V — The Aftermath & World State

The narrative climax leads to lasting consequences:

* the final status of Prometheus and the Lighthouse alters world parameters;
* changes in trade routes, hostile spawns, and safe zone configurations;
* sets the stage for future expansions and post-game live operations.

---

## 6. Detailed Chronological Matrix

| Period | Narrative Significance | Historical / World Event | Player Knowledge State |
|:-------|:----------------------:|--------------------------|------------------------|
| **Pre-Catastrophe** | Foundational Lore | Pre-EMP genetic research and Prometheus AI genesis | Unknown at start; learned via archives |
| **The Trigger** | Historical Milestone | Worldwide EMP blackout and initial contamination | Progressively investigated |
| **Phase I** | Campaign Prologue | Awakening in the Urban Ruins; survival basics | Immediate local knowledge |
| **Phase II** | Investigation | Decrypting transmissions; first faction contacts | Partial knowledge; competing accounts |
| **Phase III** | Critical Turning Point | Discovering Prometheus and Dr. Voss's laboratory | Major narrative revelations |
| **Phase IV** | Campaign Climax | Assault on the Lighthouse; factional mobilization | Direct world consequences |
| **Phase V** | Endgame World State | Campaign finale and post-catastrophe new order | Full awareness; player-driven world |

---

## 7. Timeline vs. Narrative Sequence

The objective chronology of the universe and the player's discovery sequence are deliberately decoupled.

### Objective Chronology

Represents the factual chronological order in which events occurred in the game universe.

### Narrative Chronology

Represents the specific sequence in which the player experiences, investigates, and decodes these historical events.

This structural separation maintains tension, investigative satisfaction, and dramatic irony.

---

## 8. Canon Management & Future Expansions

The project timeline is modular and continuously evolving.

When introducing new narrative content, game designers must ensure alignment across:

* Campaign scenarios
* Lore and worldbuilding documents
* Side quests and NPC dialogue
* Character biographies
* Faction ideologies
* Multiplayer live events

Any major story addition must be cataloged here once officially ratified as canon.

---

## 9. Consistency Rules

When proposing a new narrative beat or historical event:

1. Determine its exact date or chronological epoch.
2. Identify preceding events and causal triggers.
3. Map out resulting environmental and social consequences.
4. Verify there are no continuity contradictions with existing canon.
5. Define what portion of this event is visible to the player at any given level.
6. Update this document accordingly.

### Event Canon Status

* **Canon** — Officially ratified lore.
* **In Development** — Planned story beat undergoing review.
* **To Be Defined (TBD)** — Conceptual requirement whose exact lore details are pending.
* **Non-Canon / Legacy** — Deprecated draft no longer part of official lore.
