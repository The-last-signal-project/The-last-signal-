## 1. Overview

The world of **The Last Signal Online** is composed of numerous open regions that players can explore freely.

Each region features its own distinct identity and plays a vital role in the overarching worldbuilding of the game.

A region is defined by several core characteristics:

* Its environmental terrain;
* Its climate and weather conditions;
* Its contamination level;
* Its available resource pools;
* Its environmental hazards;
* Its indigenous and mutated fauna;
* Its points of interest and landmarks;
* Its historical background;
* Its dynamic events;
* Its active anomalies, if any.

Regions are designed to immerse players in a vast world authentically devastated and shaped by the global catastrophe.

---

## 2. Role of Regions

Regions structure the open world into coherent zones without restricting open-ended player exploration.

Specifically, regions serve to:

* Diversify biomes and visual environments;
* Guide player progression smoothly through difficulty scaling;
* Distribute crafting and survival resources across the map;
* Balance the spread of environmental hazards and high-tier threats;
* Tell the history and lore of the fallen civilization;
* Anchor cities, survivor settlements, and key installations;
* Create high-value discovery hotspots and secret areas;
* Deliver varied and distinct exploration gameplay experiences.

A region is far more than a simple geographical zone: it possesses a unique **narrative and environmental identity**.

## 4. Contamination Levels

Regions are heavily influenced by their ambient contamination tier.

This contamination level directly dictates:

* The types and mutation stages of creatures encountered;
* The overall gameplay difficulty and combat encounters;
* The tier and rarity of harvestable resources;
* The quality of loot and progression rewards;
* The protective gear and equipment required by players.

### 🟢 Green Zone

Green zones are the safest areas in the world.

They host primary survivor settlements and serve as the starting grounds for new players.

Creatures in these zones are relatively sparse, docile, or low-threat.

---

### 🟡 Yellow Zone

Yellow zones mark the boundary of contaminated territories.

They introduce heightened danger alongside higher-tier crafting materials and loot.

These areas act as a vital buffer and transition phase between safe zones and heavily irradiated sectors.

---

### 🔴 Red Zone

Red zones are severely contaminated.

Hostile mutants and infected threats are numerous, aggressive, and patrol in packs.

Surviving these zones demands thorough preparation, specialized protection, and high-tier equipment.

---

### ⚫ Black Zone

Black zones represent the apex of contamination and planetary devastation.

They constitute the most perilous environments in **The Last Signal Online**.

Black zones feature:

* Extremely lethal apex predators and mutated horrors;
* World bosses and endgame encounters;
* Forgotten research facilities and high-tech ruins;
* Lost pre-collapse technologies and experimental blueprints;
* Critical narrative revelations concerning the Catastrophe.

The **Mountain of the Last Signal** is located deep within a Black Zone.

## 5. Types of Regions

Regions can take a wide variety of forms and topologies.

Examples include:

* Abandoned metropolises and decaying urban centers;
* Destroyed villages and ghost towns;
* Contaminated forests and irradiated wilderness;
* Rugged mountain ranges and rocky highlands;
* Classified subterranean research facilities;
* Fortified military bases and weapon depots;
* Industrial complexes and factory parks;
* Toxic wetlands and irradiated swamps;
* Natural cave networks and cavern systems;
* Deep underground emergency bunkers.

A single region can also seamlessly integrate multiple sub-environments.

For instance, a mountainous region might encompass:

* A dense irradiated pine forest;
* A ruined pre-war alpine village;
* A decommissioned scientific research outpost;
* A labyrinth of underground caves;
* A secure government fallout shelter.

---

## 6. Exploration

The world is crafted as a fully connected, seamless open world.

Players have complete freedom to roam and explore across diverse regions.

Exploration allows players to discover:

* Raw crafting and building materials;
* Abandoned structures and architectural ruins;
* Thriving cities and hidden outposts;
* Ruined settlements and survivor enclaves;
* Advanced scientific installations;
* Sealed fallout shelters and bunkers;
* Mutated wildlife and dangerous apex beasts;
* Audio logs, research notes, and classified documents;
* Narrative secrets and hidden caches;
* Unstable anomalous phenomena;
* Enigmatic radio signals broadcasting on forgotten frequencies.

Regions do not reveal all their mysteries at once; lore fragments and hidden areas are uncovered gradually through persistent exploration.

---

## 7. Resources

Each region possesses a distinct resource distribution profile.

Key gatherable resources include:

* **Wood:** Structural lumber, fuel, and basic tool components;
* **Stone & Minerals:** Raw construction materials and masonry;
* **Metals & Alloys:** Scrap iron, refined steel, and structural metals;
* **Food & Water:** Edible rations, clean water, and consumable forage;
* **Electronic Components:** Circuit boards, microchips, and wires;
* **Fuel:** Gasoline, battery cells, and chemical energy sources;
* **Medical Supplies:** Antidotes, first-aid kits, and pharmaceuticals.

Resource abundance is strictly governed by the regional environment and historical background.

Resources are utilized for:

* Weapon and armor crafting;
* Gear repairs and maintenance;
* Base building and settlement fortification;
* Player trading and economic barter.

---

## 8. Hazards & Dangers

Every region presents unique threats to survivor survival.

Regional dangers include:

* Airborne radiation and toxic chemical contamination;
* Aggressive mutated fauna and apex monstrosities;
* Harsh environmental conditions and extreme weather;
* Structurally compromised and collapsing architecture;
* Unstable physics anomalies;
* Sudden dynamic world events and hazard surges;
* Hostile bandit patrols and rival faction ambushes.

A region's overall hazard profile is always proportional to its designated contamination tier.

---

## 9. Creatures & Mutants

The wildlife and hostile entities inhabiting a region directly reflect its ecosystem and contamination level.

Creatures originate from three primary sources:

* **Infected / Contaminated Humans:** Victims of the pathogen or radiation;
* **Mutated Animals:** Native fauna altered by genetic degradation;
* **Escaped Laboratory Experiments:** Synthetic bioweapons and failed prototypes.

Each region features a distinct ecosystem of predators, prey, and pack-hunting mutants that reinforce its thematic identity.

---

## 10. Lore & Environmental Storytelling

Every region possesses a rich history spanning both pre-collapse society and post-apocalyptic struggles.

This narrative is communicated through environmental storytelling:

* The condition and architecture of ruined buildings;
* Weathered memorials and municipal remnants;
* Abandoned personal letters, manifests, and audio logs;
* Dialogue with local survivors and scavengers;
* Relics and artifacts of the lost civilization;
* Derelict corporate and scientific research sites;
* Residual signs of past military skirmishes;
* Mysterious automated radio broadcasts.

The physical world allows observant players to piece together the sequence of the Catastrophe organically without relying solely on explicit quest dialogue.

---

## 11. Anomalies

Certain regions are warped by volatile anomalies.

Documented anomaly phenomena include:

* **Electromagnetic Storms:** Disrupt electronic HUDs, scopes, and equipment;
* **Communication Dead Zones:** Block radio chatter and radar scanning;
* **Optical & Luminescent Disruptions:** Distort vision and spatial orientation;
* **Gravitational Fluctuations:** Alter physical movement, fall physics, and projectile arcs;
* **Unstable Contamination Surges:** Shifting toxic pockets that require adaptive routing.

Anomalies dynamically modify environmental gameplay and generate unpredictable emergent encounters.

---

## 12. Inter-Regional Connections

Regions are interconnected components of a single continuous ecosystem.

They are linked through:

* Natural geographical transitions (rivers, valleys, mountain passes);
* Historical and political ties;
* Regional supply chains and economic trade routes;
* Faction territorial rivalries and border disputes;
* Cascading world events and weather patterns;
* Contamination flow and environmental spillover;
* Highway networks, railroad tracks, and survival trails;
* Long-range radio signal relays.

Major player actions within one region can trigger ripples and consequences across neighboring territories.

## 15. Regional Evolution & World Persistence

**The Last Signal Online** features a persistent, living world.

Regions undergo dynamic evolution over time through narrative progression and player agency:

* Areas can be cleared and stabilized, becoming safer over time;
* Contamination can spread or intensify following catastrophic events;
* Settlements and fortifications can be rebuilt or upgraded;
* Global world events can permanently alter regional landscapes;
* Resource nodes and harvestable pools shift based on player consumption;
* Faction wars can flip territorial ownership and patrol behavior;
* Spontaneous anomaly surges can temporarily reshape local physics and rules.

Specific progression mechanics and live evolution systems are detailed in the corresponding gameplay documents.
