## 1. Overview

Characters constitute the living actors across the world of **The Last Signal Online**.

They embody individual survivors, organized settlement populations, key narrative figures, faction leaders, and game-controlled entities, endowing the post-apocalyptic universe with depth, identity, and personal history.

Every character must organically fit within the harsh, persistent world defined in [`02_UNIVERS_ENG.md`](02_UNIVERS_ENG.md).

Characters operate with their own survival motivations, ideological convictions, social allegiances, specialized knowledge, and concrete functions within the wasteland economy.

---

## 2. Role of Characters

Characters fulfill diverse narrative, social, and systemic functions in the game:

* **Breathing Life into the World:** Transforming barren ruins and fortified settlements into believable human communities;
* **Facilitating Player Interaction:** Serving as points of contact for trade, dialogue, contracts, and medical assistance;
* **Transmitting Critical Information:** Providing intelligence on regional dangers, mutant nests, and mysterious broadcast transmissions;
* **Driving Dynamic Events:** Initiating regional defense campaigns, scavenger expeditions, and political uprisings;
* **Advancing Narrative & Lore:** Imparting personal perspectives on the pre-collapse society and the aftermath of the catastrophe;
* **Providing Essential Services:** Offering equipment repair, weapon crafting, medical treatment, and banking services;
* **Fueling the Economy:** Participating in resource trade, barter, contract bounties, and merchant networks;
* **Representing Factions:** Embodying the values, doctrines, and diplomatic tensions of the major survivor factions;
* **Enriching Regional Identities:** Differentiating outposts through unique cultural dialects, customs, and survival philosophies;
* **Shaping World History:** Reacting to persistent global events and player-driven territorial shifts.

Characters are designed not merely as decorative background fixtures, but as proactive agents within a dynamic ecosystem.

---

## 3. Character Classifications

Characters are organized into three primary operational categories:

### 3.1 Player Characters

Player Characters (PCs) are avatars directly created and controlled by human players.

Player characters actively engage in:

* Open-world exploration across hazardous biomes and underground dungeons;
* Tactical real-time PvE and PvP combat;
* Commerce, bartering, and market transactions;
* Scavenging raw materials, harvesting wildlife, and crafting equipment;
* Forming survival groups, tactical strike squads, and persistent player guilds;
* Developing character statistics, skill trees, and professional masteries;
* Participating in dynamic world events and faction territory wars;
* Interacting with fellow survivors and the NPC population.

Detailed character creation rules are documented in [`10_CREATION_PERSONNAGE_ENG.md`](10_CREATION_PERSONNAGE_ENG.md).

---

### 3.2 Non-Player Characters (NPCs)

Non-Player Characters (NPCs) are autonomous entities governed by the game's artificial intelligence and behavior systems.

Common NPC roles include:

* **Settlers & Civilians:** Laborers, farmers, scavengers, and regular enclave inhabitants;
* **Merchants & Barterers:** Trading general goods, ammunition, provisions, and regional scrap;
* **Specialized Artisans:** Blacksmiths, armorers, gunsmiths, and electronic technicians;
* **Field Medics & Doctors:** Healing injuries, treating infections, and administering anti-rad cures;
* **Scouts & Informants:** Selling regional maps, danger warnings, and anomaly coordinates;
* **Enclave Guards & Militia:** Patrolling perimeter fences and defending settlements against mutant raids;
* **Quest-Givers & Coordinators:** Assigning salvage contracts, bounty hunts, and recon missions;
* **Hostile Combatants:** Renegade bandits, raiders, rogue mercenaries, and fanatical cultists.

NPC behaviors dynamically reflect their current role, environmental danger level, and faction alignment.

---

### 3.3 Key Story Characters

Key Characters are prominent narrative figures who drive the core storyline and geopolitical fate of the world.

They are linked directly to:

* The central campaign questline (*"The Mystery of the Lighthouse"*);
* Faction leadership councils and diplomatic command structures;
* Major settlement governorships and military garrisons;
* Epochal world events, scientific breakthroughs, and historical investigations;
* Ancient secrets regarding the origins of **The Last Signal**.

These characters feature deeply developed personal backstories, unique voice lines, branching dialogue trees, and bespoke reputation mechanics.

---

## 4. Player Characters

The player character represents the survivor's personal avatar and agency inside the persistent world.

As players navigate the wasteland, their character develops along several progression vectors:

* **Attributes & Base Statistics:** Physical endurance, strength, agility, intelligence, and survival resilience;
* **Combat & Survival Skills:** Weapon proficiencies, stealth, medical treatment, and hazard resistance;
* **Crafting & Harvesting Professions:** Engineering, chemistry, metallurgy, hunting, and electronic repair;
* **Faction Reputation:** Positive standing unlocking high-tier faction gear, or hostility triggering bounty hits.

Player characters are fully integrated into the world rules, subject to hunger, radiation, injury, and environmental hazards.

---

## 5. Non-Player Characters (NPCs)

NPCs are designed with consistent daily routines and situational awareness.

Every NPC profile defines:

* **Functional Role:** Primary occupation and service offered;
* **Base Location:** Residence, shop counter, or assigned defensive patrol route;
* **Daily Schedule:** Dynamic transitions between work, social interaction, and rest;
* **Social Connections:** Family bonds, business partnerships, and ideological rivalries;
* **Faction Allegiance:** Direct loyalty ties to regional factions or independent communities;
* **Intellectual Scope:** What specific lore, rumors, and navigation data they possess;
* **Behavioral Archetype:** Combat posture, cowardice, aggression, or defensive retreat triggers.

While some NPCs remain stationed at vital settlement facilities, roaming NPCs (such as trade caravans, courier squads, and hunting parties) traverse inter-regional road networks.

---

## 6. Key Story Characters

Key story characters possess distinctive identities and purpose-driven motivations.

Their narrative roles include:

* Unveiling major campaign plot twists and strategic directives;
* Introducing players to unfamiliar wilderness zones and hostile territories;
* Representing the ideological core of conflicting factions;
* Dispensing rare technological blueprints and experimental prototype gear;
* Triggering multi-stage epic quest arcs and instanced storyline missions;
* Serving as pivotal moral arbiters whose survival or death impacts the regional balance of power.

Their presence and actions are rigorously justified within the historical timeline and world lore.

---

## 7. Identity & Profile Characteristics

Every documented character profile includes the following core identity descriptors:

### General Identity Data

* **Unique Identifier:** Alphanumeric registry code (`PERS-XXX`);
* **Full Name:** Given name, family surname, or wasteland moniker;
* **Classification:** Player, Standard NPC, or Key Story NPC;
* **Operational Role:** Merchant, Medic, Faction Leader, Guard Captain, etc.;
* **Origin & Background:** Birthplace, former pre-war profession, or post-collapse background;
* **Current Location:** Region, settlement name, specific building, or patrol coordinates;
* **Faction Alignment:** Formal faction membership or independent alignment status.

### Extended Profile Data

Major and narrative characters additionally possess:

* **Personal Backstory:** Narrative history explaining their journey and survival;
* **Core Objectives:** Immediate priorities and long-term ambitions;
* **Knowledge Vault:** Specific encrypted data, secrets, or quest information they hold;
* **Dialogue Matrix:** Branching conversational trees reacting to player reputation;
* **Tactical Stats & Loadout:** Weapons, combat gear, health pool, and special combat perks.

---

## 8. Personality & Behavioral Profiles

Characters are differentiated through distinct psychological traits and behavioral tendencies.

NPC responses depend on:

* Their innate personality archetype;
* The safety and contamination tier of their surrounding environment;
* Their historical experience with rival factions;
* The player's current reputation and faction standing;
* Active global emergencies and dynamic settlement events.

Behavioral Archetypes include:

* **Welcoming & Altruistic:** Open to assisting strangers, providing medical aid, and offering fair trade;
* **Suspicious & Guarded:** Demanding identification, charging higher prices, and keeping weapons drawn;
* **Pragmatic & Neutral:** Solely focused on survival, barter value, and immediate self-interest;
* **Hostile & Aggressive:** Attacking on sight or issuing immediate ultimatums;
* **Opportunistic:** Ready to betray alliances or exploit power vacuums for personal gain;
* **Protective & Devoted:** Prioritizing the defense of their community and allies at all costs.

---

## 9. Relationships & Social Dynamics

Characters maintain complex relational networks among themselves and toward players.

Relational vectors include:

* **Trust & Respect:** Earned through completed tasks, shared defense, and honest commerce;
* **Suspicion & Distrust:** Triggered by conflicting faction ties or trespassing;
* **Alliances & Pacts:** Formal treaties binding merchants, militias, and political leaders;
* **Blood Debts & Vendettas:** Longstanding rivalries that can spark spontaneous skirmishes;
* **Economic Dependencies:** Supply chains connecting resource gatherers, craftsmen, and distributors.

Player actions directly influence these relationships, opening new narrative avenues or closing off entire settlement hubs.

---

## 10. Factions & Affiliations

Character allegiances anchor them to the geopolitical struggles of the world.

Faction affiliation influences:

* **Ideological Stance:** How the character views the pre-collapse world and future civilization;
* **Rules of Engagement:** Who the character considers an ally, a neutral trading partner, or an enemy;
* **Commercial Inventory:** Access to faction-exclusive ammunition, medical formulas, and weapons;
* **Dialogue Availability:** Exclusive storylines unlocked only at high faction standing.

Faction memberships must strictly align with the doctrines defined in [`05_FACTIONS_ENG.md`](05_FACTIONS_ENG.md).

---

## 11. World & Ecosystem Integration

Every character must have a logical and sustainable place within the wasteland ecosystem:

* Inhabitants of farming settlements engage in agriculture, water purification, and livestock care;
* Outpost guards patrol fortified walls and maintain defensive floodlights;
* Industrial salvage crews dismantle vehicle wrecks and extract copper wiring;
* Researchers operate diagnostic terminals in shielded cleanrooms.

Their spoken dialogues, visible equipment, and daily routines must reflect the physical realities and scarcity of their specific region.

---

## 12. Character Evolution & Persistence

In a persistent living world, key characters and NPCs are not entirely static:

* **Dynamic Relocation:** Characters may flee compromised settlements or migrate to new trading hubs;
* **Political Promotion:** Subordinates may rise to leadership following the death or exile of a faction commander;
* **Economic Adaptation:** Merchant stock dynamically adjusts based on regional supply shortages;
* **Behavioral Shifts:** Characters may become hardened, traumatized, or hostile following major community losses during world events.

All character evolutions must maintain coherence with ongoing server state and narrative arcs.

---

## 13. Narrative Integration & Lore Delivery

Characters serve as primary storytellers in **The Last Signal Online**.

Through their interactions, players uncover:

* The chronological history of the collapse following the global EMP;
* Eyewitness accounts of societal breakdown and biological mutations;
* The founding ideals and dark secrets of emerging survivor factions;
* The hidden locations of lost underground bunkers and forgotten research labs;
* Speculations, scientific theories, and religious myths regarding the **Radio Signal from The Lighthouse**.

To ensure immersive worldbuilding, character dialogue is complemented by environmental storytelling, salvageable audio logs, and physical documents.
